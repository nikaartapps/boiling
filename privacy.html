Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Linematya is a line boil drawing app for iPad: you draw a still picture and its lines boil like hand-drawn animation. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: it doesn’t collect any.

Data we collect

None. Linematya does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Your drawings

Everything you draw stays on your device, stored by the app in its own storage on your iPad. We have no access to it and never receive a copy. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your device backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Content you export (PNG, APNG, GIF, or video) leaves the app only when you choose to save or share it, and goes wherever you send it.

Photos

A picture you import is copied into your drawing on your device and goes nowhere else. The app asks to add to your photo library only so it can save an export when you choose to; it never reads your library on its own.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. It is used only to answer you, is never shared or sold, and is deleted when the conversation is no longer needed.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
